# Three-minute demonstration

1. Open the primary web app. Briefly state the goal: help newcomers in BC leave with a justified next step.
2. Choose “Try a demo”. Point out that Mei is fictional and draft logic is labelled. BC NSP needs institutional service information; the federal route keeps exceptions open; StudentAid BC shows the proposed limited status check.
3. Select “Review answers”. Change “Institution offers the service” from unknown to “No, it does not”; save. Show the BC NSP draft state changing, the version notice, and the revised enquiry. The other two results retain their state.
4. Open “Why this next step”, then the official-source details. Show the rule ID, hash, snapshot check and independent-review boundary.
5. Edit the enquiry, tick a checklist item, and download the plan. Nothing is sent.
6. Open About VERA and select Tomas's uncertainty demo. The economic-PR time-limit case stops at official review; there is no calculated cutoff date.
7. Mention the measured regression results and remaining independent review in `docs/RELEASE.md`. Credit Diya Giri and Kopal Chaudhary.

Deterministic fallback: `python demo_rules.py`. The local browser version also works without a model provider. Screenshots are included as QA evidence. A narrated demonstration video has not been recorded.
